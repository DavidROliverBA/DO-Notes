# F34 — A new name, one syllable, and an icon to go with it (done 2026-11-01: Skein)

**Size** M · **Evidence** [GAP-ANALYSIS.md](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md) (2026-11-01): "Commonplace" is four
syllables, slow to say to Siri ("save to Commonplace"), shared with many other products, and the
icon is a plain mark that doesn't stand out on a home screen or in the Dock

A short, catchy name and a striking icon designed together, before TestFlight puts the name into
the App Store record. Candidates (each needs a trademark, App Store and domain check before it's
chosen):

| Name | Why it fits | The icon |
|---|---|---|
| **Cairn** (recommended) | A pile of stones that walkers add to as they pass, marking the way: a library many hands build, one note at a time | Three stacked stones, the top one in the accent colour, a fine line joining them like a link |
| **Quire** | A gathering of folded pages, how books were bound; a commonplace book was made of them | Folded sheets fanned open, their edges catching the light |
| **Sheaf** | Gathered and bound together: notes collected into one | A bundle of pages tied at the middle, flaring at both ends |
| **Weft** | The thread woven across the cloth, binding it: links through your notes | A woven square with one bright thread running through |
| **Nook** | A quiet corner of your own to think in | An arched alcove holding a single open page |

Ruled out: Glean, Hive, Loom (well-known products already).

**Checked 2026-11-01 (a web search, not a registry search):** both names are crowded among notes
apps. **Wren:** an iPhone notes app for students (formerly NoteNest), a Windows sticky-notes app
(Markdown), small open-source Markdown editors, Wren AI (a data tool), and *Wren* is taken on
the App Store. **Cairn:** Cairn Notes (open-source Markdown notes with sync, Linux, Windows and
Android), several AI notes projects on GitHub, a Mac App Store app called Cairn, and a hiking
app. Neither showed a live US registration for software, but the UK, EU and US registers
(classes 9 and 42) still need checking directly. Respellings (Wrenn, Kairn) make the name harder
for Siri to hear. If a name goes ahead, the likely shape is the name on the home screen (the
app's display name) and a longer, unique one in App Store Connect, such as "Wren Library",
since App Store names must be unique.

**Checked later the same day (two syllables, if the rule bends):** **Commonplace** itself is the
most crowded (five or more iPhone apps, most of them notes or quote journals, and descriptive as
a trademark). **Graphbook**: an open-source AI pipeline tool, an iPhone maths app and
graphbook.app. **Threadnote**: five notes apps (two on iPhone, one on Android, a Windows app and
a developer tool), and threadnote.com and .io are taken. **Threadbook** is the cleanest found:
the only product is ThreadBook, a sewing-thread organiser on iPhone and Android (a different
field), but that store name is taken (so "Threadbook Notes" or similar in App Store Connect);
threadbook.com and .co.uk are registered, threadbook.io looked free.

**Rare and made-up one-syllable names, checked the same day:** **Skein** (a coil of thread; also
geese flying in a line) is clear of notes apps, with only two knitting apps. **Thrum** (the loose
threads left on a loom) has only a meditation app. **Glim** (an old word for a light) has a
group-tasks app. Out: **Scrive** (a large e-signature company), **Nem** (Némos, an AI notes app
named after memory), **Vell** (VelNotes and Velora, both notes apps). WrenNotes is out too (the
iPhone app "Wren: AI Notes"). An unusual name can be taught to Siri with `INAlternativeAppNames`.

**Done when:** a name is chosen with David; the icon is drawn as SVG (light, dark and tinted iOS
variants, and a Mac version with depth) and passes at 16 px; the shown name changes everywhere a
person sees it (the page title, the web manifest, the iPhone and Mac apps' display names, the
share sheet, Siri's phrases (they follow the app's name), the tour, help, README, the standalone
file and published sites' "made with"); and `bun run screens` and `bun run help:shots` are
retaken. What keeps the old name, so nothing breaks: the repository, bundle ids, the
`commonplace://` scheme (the new one is added beside it), the database, API routes, storage keys,
the Render service and its address.

**Chosen 2026-11-01: Skein**, with icon C (a ball of thread whose strand loops once and runs out
to a gold link), in shades of navy blue. Still for David to do: check the UK IPO, EUIPO and USPTO registers (classes 9
and 42), and choose the App Store Connect name (unique, such as "Skein Notes").
