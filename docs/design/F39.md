# F39 — Notifications and activity (done 2026-11-01)

**Why:** the [gap analysis](../GAP-ANALYSIS.md): in a shared collection nothing told you that
someone mentioned you, invited you or changed what you care about.

**Decided with David (2026-11-01):**
- **Where:** a bell in Skein, plus alerts from the iPhone and Mac apps, fetched when they
  refresh (no push service, no Apple key; an alert can wait until the next refresh).
- **What:** mentions, invitations (and acceptances), notes you follow (at most once an hour per
  note), and comments (L3).
- **@people:** typing `@` lists the collection's members and your assistants in one list.

**How it works:** `src/notifications.ts`. `notifications` rows per person (the newest 200 kept);
`note_follows`; `vault_seen` for *Since you last looked*. Mentions are found after each save by
comparing the note before and after (`notes.persist`, `createNote`): a member named in the new
text (`@First`, `@Full Name`, `[[Full Name]]`, as reminders read them) who wasn't before is told,
never the writer. Followers are told unless they were told about that note in the last hour.
Invitations notify the invitee only when they have an account (the inviter sees the same reply
either way, as N13 requires). The web client checks every minute and when the window comes back;
the apps (`ios/Shared/Alerts.swift`) show unread ones newer than the last they showed, at most
five at a time, starting from "now" on a new device.
