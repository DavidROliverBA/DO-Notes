## Release 2, as built

- **Day One import** (`src/dayone.ts`, `POST /api/vaults/:v/journal/import`, editor or better):
  reads Day One's JSON export (a zip with one `.json` per journal, and `photos/`, `videos/`,
  `audios/`, `pdfs/` named by md5) or a single `.json`. Each entry goes to its day's daily note
  under `## Journal`, at its local time in its own `timeZone`. Day One's `# Title` lines become
  bold (a heading would end the Journal section), its backslash escapes are removed, tags follow
  as `#Tag-Name`, and `dayone-moment://` attachments are stored once under `attachments/Day One`
  and embedded. Location, weather, stars and empty entries are dropped. An entry already in its
  day with the same time and text is skipped, so a second import adds nothing. One write per
  day, through `createNote` / `overwriteContent` with a function. A dry run counts first, and
  the app asks before importing. Checked against a real export (383 entries in two journals,
  2017–2025: 359 imported over 225 days, 24 empty), every entry reading back unchanged.
- **Reflect on this week** (⌘K): the home collection's last seven days of entries, oldest
  first, with a request to sum up, name themes, note what went well and what weighed, ask a
  question or two, and change nothing. It starts a new conversation in the side panel
  (`askInPanel` in `AiPanel.tsx`).
